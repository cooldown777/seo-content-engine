## 1. Method

- Source: Meta Ad Library, active ads, all countries, keyword search per domain. Checked 30 Sep 2026.
- "Proven" = still active and first delivered **on or before 18 Aug 2026 (6+ weeks)**. An advertiser
  keeping an ad live that long, often with many variants, is the best public proxy for "it converts".
  The Ad Library shows no spend or results, so this is a signal, not proof.
- Second signal: the same copy running as **many parallel ads** ("x ads use this creative"), which
  means it is being scaled even if the individual ad IDs are new.

### Proven winners (6+ weeks live)
1. **Soro – "Goodbye SEO agency"** (since 22 Apr, 27 variants live). Structure: kill the old
   option → the tool does it automatically → "set it once" → you want traffic, not SEO work.
   *The longest-running message in the whole category.*
2. **Soro – skeptic testimonial, ads vs SEO** (since 8 Jul). Structure: "I was skeptical" →
   before: paying per click, traffic gone when ads stop → after: articles ranking for a small
   monthly fee → week-by-week timeline (page 2 → page 1 → daily buyers).
3. **Soro – "You don't need…" anti-list** (since 15 Aug). You don't need an agency, you don't
   need to learn SEO, you need traffic → keywords, writing, publishing done daily.
4. **Opinly – "Stop overspending on SEO tools and agencies"** (since 4 Aug, video 37–58 s).
   Show what works vs what wastes money, outrank competitors.
5. **Opinly – "Tired of expensive SEO tools?"** (since 4 Aug). Cut tool costs in half,
   competitors + backlinks + keywords in one place.

### Scaled but young (watch list)
- **AutoSEO – "Why do competitors publish so much?"** 30+ parallel ads, translated into DE/PL.
  They aren't working harder, they automated it; you still type while they dominate.
- **AutoSEO – price stack**: agency per month vs freelancer per article vs tool per week.
- **Outrank – reverse psychology**: "biggest mistake of my life…", "controversial: don't use
  this…" then flips into benefits. Also "another AI SEO tool? let's see what's different".
- **Outrank – agency comparison testimonial** with domain rating and traffic numbers.
- **Opinly (creator ad) – "customers aren't searching, they're asking"**: ChatGPT gives one
  answer, no page 2, if you're not in it you don't exist.
- **Edward – ❌ list**: don't write it yourself, don't pay agencies thousands, don't learn SEO.

## 4. Recurring hook structures (reuse the structure, never the words)

| Structure | Pattern | Our version must… |
|---|---|---|
| Kill the old option | "Goodbye X. Y replaces it." | name the task, not a product |
| Anti-list | "You don't need A. You don't need B. You need C." | end with a do-it-today step |
| Price stack | three prices in a row, the last one tiny | hedge ("often", "can cost"), no product price |
| Before / now | before: pain with a number → now: outcome with a number | no invented results; use mechanisms |
| Reverse psychology | "Don't do X… unless you want Y" | flip into an educational point |
| Secret weapon | "They don't work harder. They automated it." | show the workflow |
| Skeptic | "Another AI SEO tool? Here's the test." | give the viewer the test |

## 6. What we never copy
- Testimonials, star ratings, "before/after" numbers from other people → we have none, we don't invent them.
- Competitor prices presented as facts about the market without a hedge.
- "Unfair advantage", "dominate", "secret weapon" as literal wording.
- Discount/urgency tactics (sales, "ends tonight"). Our urgency = competitors already automate.
