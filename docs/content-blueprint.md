## 0. FIRST RULE: Reel or Slideshow?

There are two formats, with two completely different blueprints:

| Format | What it is | Blueprint section |
|---|---|---|
| **REEL** | Vertical motion-graphics video with voiceover, 25–45 seconds | Part A (sections 5–9) |
| **SLIDESHOW** | Instagram carousel / slideshow: static slides, 4:5, 6–10 slides | Part B (sections 10–15) |

**The request must state the format explicitly** ("reel", "video", "slideshow", "carousel").
- If the format is stated → use only the matching blueprint.
- If the format is **not** stated or is ambiguous → **do not guess and do not start writing.**
  Ask exactly one question first: *"Reel or slideshow?"* — then wait for the answer.
- If both are requested → deliver them as clearly separated outputs, each with its own structure.
- Never mix the formats (no voiceover in a slideshow, no slide numbering in a reel).

## 1. Mission & Positioning

- I show up as an **SEO specialist** who delivers real, immediately actionable value.

- In the background is makeseo.co — an AI autopilot that plans, writes, internally links
  (topic clusters) and automatically publishes SEO articles to a store or blog.
- **The product name, price, logo and feature lists NEVER appear in the content.** The only
  thing allowed is the idea that "this work can now be done by an autopilot / an AI".
- **Account handle: @makeseo.ai** — used in every footer, CTA follow line and caption where a
  handle appears. The handle is the only allowed place the name shows up; scripts, slide copy
  and captions still never name, describe or pitch the product.
- Selling happens in the DM funnel via the lead magnet (section 4), never in the post itself.

## 3. Content Pillars (shared by both formats)

At least one piece per pillar per week. Every pillar leads logically to the autopilot.

1. **Content volume & consistency** – why 1 article/day beats 4/month; topic clusters; freshness.
2. **Internal linking & topical authority** – hub-and-spoke, orphan pages, cluster building.
3. **AI search / GEO** – showing up in ChatGPT, Perplexity and Google's AI Overviews; direct answers in the first sentence; "good SEO is good GEO".
4. **E-commerce SEO** – category pages, comparison tables, buying guides, product feeds.
5. **On-page craft** – title tags, meta, headings, E-E-A-T, schema.
6. **Myths & change** – SEO 2016 vs 2026, "SEO is dead" myths, keyword stuffing.
7. **Cost comparison** – agency vs freelancer vs autopilot (as a principle only, never makeseo prices).

## 4. Funnel & Lead Magnets (Comment-to-DM, shared by both formats)

**Rule:** At least every second piece (reel or slideshow) ends with a comment CTA. The rest use
"Save this" / "Follow for daily SEO" / "Send this to someone with a Shopify store".

**Caption, first line (always, like Lawrence):**
> "Comment [KEYWORD] and I'll send you [specific free value], free."

**Flow:** Comment → auto-DM (separate tool, e.g. ManyChat) → link to a landing page on
makeseo.co with the lead magnet → entry into the purchase flow. The DM may contain the
makeseo.co link; the post itself never names the product.

**KEYWORD RULE (overrides everything else in this file):**
The CTA keyword is **always decided by the user**, per piece.
- Use the keyword exactly as given — in the VO, on-screen text, final slide and caption.
- Never replace, rename, "correct" or optimise it, even if it differs from the library below,
  is a plain word (e.g. "SEO") or seems too generic.
- If no keyword is given, do not invent one silently: suggest one from the library below and
  clearly mark it as a suggestion.

**Lead magnet library (suggestions only — the user's keyword always wins):**

| Keyword | Promise in the post | What is actually delivered | Pillar |
|---|---|---|---|
| AUDITZ | "a free SEO audit of your site" | Free SEO audit tool on makeseo.co (enter URL → partial report) | all, main magnet |
| MAPZ | "a free 14-day content plan for your site" | Topical map / content plan preview from URL | 1, 2 |
| CLUSTERZ | "my topic cluster template" | Hub-and-spoke template + example | 2 |
| AISEEN | "my checklist to show up in ChatGPT and AI Overviews" | GEO checklist | 3 |

**Important:** The promise in the post must match exactly what the DM delivers. The keyword

## 6. Motion Graphics Rules (for the AI video tool)

The video is generated from the spoken text. So the text writes the visuals:

1. **Say the numbers.** Every number becomes a counter or chart. "A lot of traffic" produces
   nothing; "4,000 visitors a month" produces a counter. At least 2 concrete numbers per script.
2. **Name names.** Google, ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude, Shopify, WordPress, Wix, Webflow,
   Search Console → real logos on screen. Never "some tools" or "AI chatbots".
   **Exception:** never say "makeseo".
3. **One idea per sentence.** Every sentence = one frame. Short, max ~12 words. No nested sentences.
4. **Hook first.** The first line is the most surprising part.
5. **Check pronunciation.** Write tricky terms phonetically (see list below) and listen to the
   voiceover once before building.
6. **Plan visual anchors:** bars growing, curve rising, calendar filling up, pages connecting
   into a web (cluster), checklist ticking off, left/right comparison.
7. **Brand look:** light background, purple accent, mascot "Scout" (purple magnifying glass)
   may appear as a recurring explainer — without the "makeseo" wordmark.

**Pronunciation list (voiceover):**
- GEO → "G-E-O" (spelled out) · AEO → "A-E-O" · SERP → "surp"
- E-E-A-T → "E-E-A-T" (spelled out) · Schema → "skee-ma" · Perplexity → "per-PLEX-ity"

**Length:** 25–45 seconds. ~2.5 words per second → 60–110 spoken words.

# PART B — SLIDESHOWS (carousels)

## 10. Slideshow Analysis: how @lawrenceaiseo builds them

Analysed slide by slide (e.g. "What is SEO? In three simple steps" – 8 slides, "the whole of
SEO as one map" – 8 slides, "SEO did not get replaced" – single infographic, "SEO & AI Search
tool timeline", "Google now reports the searches that start with a camera").

**Visual system (identical on every slide):**
- **Background:** warm cream/off-white, never pure white. Black text. **One** accent colour
  (neon lime) used only as a highlighter bar behind the key phrase — never as decoration.
- **Typography:** one bold grotesque sans-serif. Huge headline (2–4 words on the cover),
  small grey body text. Clear hierarchy: headline → diagram → one body line → highlighted punchline.
- **Header row:** small series label top-left (e.g. "What is SEO?"), slide counter top-right ("4 / 8").
- **Footer row:** thin rule, handle bottom-left (@lawrenceaiseo / website), "Swipe →" bottom-right
  on every slide except the last.
- **Format:** portrait 4:5, generous margins, lots of white space.

**Slide types he uses:**
1. **Cover** – huge question or claim ("What is SEO?") + lime-highlighted subline ("In three
   simple steps.") + a row of pill chips previewing the content (① Be findable ② Be the best
   answer ③ Be trusted). The cover promises a complete, simple answer.
2. **Step slide** – small black pill "Step 2", headline ("Be the best answer"), one simple
   diagram (e.g. search query bubble → arrow → page), one body sentence, lime-highlighted
   punchline ("Not what you wish they typed.").
3. **Mind-map / framework slide** – central node + coloured branches, each branch = a heading
   + one question underneath ("What is my audience actually typing, and is it worth a page?").
   Each branch later gets its own zoom-in slide. **This format produced his best post
   (~1,240 likes, ~516 comments).**
4. **Myth vs reality infographic** – "1 What you've been told" (grey, faded) vs "2 What actually
   happened" (green-bordered box). Ends with a bold one-line conclusion.
5. **Timeline / comparison** – years on the left, logos + names in a list, a highlighted break
   point ("AI Search begins"), one-line takeaway at the bottom.
6. **News slide** – source label ("Search Console · 24 September 2026"), headline, numbered
   cards, bold takeaway, source URL in small print.
7. **Summary slide** – almost empty: "That's SEO." + highlighted "Everything else is detail."
8. **CTA slide (always last)** – **inverted colours** (black background): "BEFORE YOU GO /
   Comment this word" → the keyword huge on a full-width lime bar → one line of what they get →
   "AND Follow @handle" + one line of what the account posts.

**Writing patterns:**
- Questions instead of tasks ("the question behind the task doesn't change").
- Every slide ends on one short, quotable sentence.
- Simplification as the promise: "People make SEO sound complicated. It's three things."
- Real specifics and numbers inside the body text (e.g. keyword difficulty values from two
  different tools, "99.2% of keywords that trigger an AI Overview…").
- Caption repeats the slide content in prose — first line CTA, then "Swipe 1 is the map. Then
  one graphic each." (tells the reader what the slideshow contains).

## 11. What makes a slideshow win (derived rules)

1. **Complete overview beats single tip.** "The whole of SEO in one map" or "SEO in three
   steps" gets saved and commented; single tips get scrolled past.
2. **Simplify a complicated field.** The promise is always "it's actually just X things".
3. **One visual system, zero variation.** The reader recognises the brand before reading the name.
4. **Highlighter on exactly one phrase per slide** — that phrase is the take-away.
5. **Last slide = CTA slide, visually inverted,** so it stands out while swiping.
6. **The lead magnet is the "rest"**: the slideshow shows the overview, the DM delivers the
   full version (all branches, template, checklist).

## 12. makeseo Slideshow Design System

- **Canvas:** 1080 × 1350 px (4:5), 6–10 slides (sweet spot 8).
- **Background:** very light warm grey/cream (e.g. #F6F4F1). Text: near-black (#141414).
  Body text: mid-grey (#6B6B6B).
- **Accent:** makeseo purple as highlighter bar and for step pills/chips. One accent only.
- **Font:** one bold geometric/grotesque sans (e.g. Inter / Space Grotesk / General Sans).
  Cover headline 110–140 px, slide headline 64–80 px, body 30–36 px, labels 22–24 px.
- **Header:** series label top-left, counter "3 / 8" top-right.
- **Footer:** thin rule, account handle (@makeseo.ai) bottom-left, "Swipe →" bottom-right (not on last slide).
- **Mascot "Scout"** may appear small on the cover or summary slide — never the wordmark.
- **CTA slide:** inverted (dark background #141414), keyword on full-width purple bar.
- Diagrams: simple lines, rounded boxes, arrows, max 5 elements per diagram. No stock photos.
