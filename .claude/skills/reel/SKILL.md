# Reel skill (from the makeseo reel rules; these win over the blueprint)

## Voice
Tech creator who discovered something useful and shares it on request. Never "my / mine /
our / I built / I made / we built", never "my skill/tool/checklist". Use "this SEO skill",
"this tool", "Someone packed the whole process into a free SEO skill for Claude."
CTA framed as a favour: "Want it? Comment SKILL and I'll send you the link."

## Hook
- Contains the word "SEO", never the word "blog". Max 12 words. First spoken line = first frame.
- About the viewer (their pain, site, money). Exactly one pattern, noted in the header:
  Contrast · Hyper-specificity · Timeframe tension · POV · or a template:
  "What nobody tells you about [X]", "Everyone is talking about [X], but here's the part
  you're missing", "Is [X] really worth it?", "The biggest mistake people make with [X]",
  "Why your SEO isn't working (and what to do instead)", "Still frustrated by [X]?"
- AI-radar hooks tie the release to SEO: "Anthropic just released [model]. Here's what it
  means for your SEO."

## Script rules
- ~50 s, 140–160 spoken words. Every sentence = one idea = one frame, max ~12 words.
- At least 2 spoken numbers and 1 real brand/tool (Google, ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude,
  Codex, Gemini, Shopify, WordPress, GitHub…). Never "makeseo".
- One topic, one example, one lesson. Tone: Confident / Skeptical / Empowering (note it).
- Hedge market claims; never invent traffic, case studies or results.
- Pronunciation: GEO "G-E-O", AEO "A-E-O", SERP "surp", E-E-A-T spelled out,
  Schema "skee-ma", Perplexity "per-PLEX-ity".

## Structure
```
FORMAT: REEL
TITLE: …
PILLAR: [1–8]
HOOK FORMULA: …
TONE: …
LENGTH: ~50s
SPOKEN WORDS: [140–160]
CTA TYPE: Comment [KEYWORD]

[0:00–0:03] HOOK        VO / ON-SCREEN / VISUAL
[0:03–0:15] VALUE BOMB  VO (one line per frame) / VISUAL
[0:15–0:40] PROOF       VO (real brand, real numbers from sources.md) / VISUAL
[0:40–0:50] CTA         VO: "Comment [KEYWORD] and I'll send you [promise]."
                        ON-SCREEN: "Comment [KEYWORD]" (hold ≥2 s)

PRONUNCIATION NOTES: …
CAPTION: line 1 = CTA, 3–5 short paragraphs, 5–8 hashtags
```
Keyword and promise only from config.json → approved_keywords.
