Google just changed its guidance on AI-written SEO content. The page was updated on October 1, 2026, and it now says it is "critical" to manually fact-check and review all AI-generated content before publishing.

The review is not only about the text. Google names titles, meta descriptions, structured data and image alt text, because all of them can show up in search results.

The 3-step check: verify every name, number and date against a real source. Make sure the title, description and alt text match the page. Then ask if the page adds real value, since Google's spam policy says many AI pages without added value can count as scaled content abuse.

Using AI is fine. Skipping the review can be the risk.
