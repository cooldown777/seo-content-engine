# Sources – 2026-10-04 (Google's AI content guidance update, reel + carousel)

- Google Search Central, "Google Search's guidance on using generative AI content on your website",
  page last updated 2026-10-01 UTC. Quotes used: "It is critical to manually factcheck and review all
  AI-generated content for accuracy and trustworthiness before publishing." · "This review also applies to
  metadata like <title> elements, meta description elements, structured data, and alternate texts for
  images, which can appear in Search results." · "generative AI outputs may contain inaccuracies (also
  known as hallucinations)." · "Sharing information about how a piece of content was created can help
  give your readers more context." –
  https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/using-gen-ai-content
- Secondary (change confirmed + "critical" wording is new): Search Engine Roundtable, Oct 2, 2026 –
  https://www.seroundtable.com/google-updates-ai-content-guidelines-factcheck-review-42217.html
- Scaled content abuse: "many pages are generated for the primary purpose of manipulating search rankings
  and not helping users"; example "Using generative AI tools or other similar tools to generate many pages
  without adding value for users" – Google spam policies:
  https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials/spam-policies
- Examples (launch year 2024 vs 2025, "Best prices in town", "Free shipping worldwide" vs 3 countries,
