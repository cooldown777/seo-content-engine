Slide 1 is the overview. Then one internal-linking SEO check per slide, each with an action and an example.

Google says it primarily finds pages through links from pages it already crawled. Its advice: every page you care about should have a link from at least one other page on your site. A new product page that no category or guide links to can be hard to find.

Start in Google Search Console: Links → Top linked pages (internal). Click a key page to see which pages link to it.

Then check how the links are built. Google can generally only crawl a link that is an a element with an href attribute, so look at menus, filters and "View all" buttons.

Make the anchor text descriptive. Google lists "click here" and "read more" as weak examples. Read only the link text: does it make sense on its own?

Finally, link in context from 2–3 related pages, and don't chain links right next to each other. Save this for your next site check.
